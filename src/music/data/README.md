# Lick data

`wjazzd-licks.json` holds phrases extracted from the **Weimar Jazz Database**
(Jazzomat Research Project, Hochschule für Musik Franz Liszt Weimar;
https://jazzomat.hfm-weimar.de). The Weimar Jazz Database is made available
under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0, and so is this extract:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

What was done to it: each solo's annotated phrases of 6–14 notes in 4/4 were
kept when every chord under them stays in the solo's key (diatonic, borrowed,
or a brief secondary dominant). Pitches are stored as semitones from the key's
tonic, rhythms as beat fractions, and the chords as roman numerals with
voicings. At most three phrases per solo; each keeps its performer and title.
